# Fresh: the service behind the £20

Fresh is "we keep an eye on it for you", not just "more edits". A client who never changes anything should still feel looked after. Target effort: **about 5 minutes per client per month** on top of edits. If it runs longer, simplify it.

## 1. Monthly pulse (WhatsApp, first week of the month)

**Data (both free, no cookies, no consent banner needed):**
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics**: add each client site (beacon script works on any host, including GitHub Pages). One dashboard for all clients; data is also available via the GraphQL API if you script it later.
- **Google Search Console**: verify each client domain at build time (already SOP step 10). Gives searches shown and clicks.
- Umami stays on getpresented.co.uk only (free plan limits sites and has no API).
- Not available from these: taps on call/WhatsApp buttons. Don't promise them.

**Template (plain, 4 lines, honest if numbers are small):**
> Hi [name], here's how [business] did in [month] 👋
> 👀 [N] visits to your site
> 🔍 Showed up in [N] Google searches, [N] clicks
> 📄 Most-viewed page: [page]
> One idea: [a specific, small change]. Want me to do it?

First month: say it's the baseline. Low numbers: lead with the idea, not the number. Never send automatically; a human reads it first.

## 2. Seasonal nudges (4 a year, batch-send)
Late March (Easter, spring hours) · mid June (summer hours, holidays) · early September (autumn, back to school) · mid November (Christmas hours, offers). One template each, sent to every Fresh client the same day: *"Christmas is coming. Any change to your opening hours or offers? Just reply with what you want."* The reply is a normal edit.

## 3. Yearly check-up (on the client's start-date anniversary)
Click through every link and button, check phone/email/address/hours, check photos and speed, confirm domain renewal and Google listing details. Reply with a short "all good / here's what I fixed" message. Then offer the flyer/card/menu refresh.

## 4. Optional extras (not promised on the site yet)
- Keep the Google Business Profile in step when hours/phone change (needs the client to add you as a manager).
- A "leave us a Google review" link and QR file on joining Fresh.
- Uptime alert (free monitor) that pings only when a client site is down.
- Properly Presented clients get this same service for their first 3 months (Fresh-level perk).
